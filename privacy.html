Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Good Steward Network

At Good Steward, we believe in transparency, honesty and integrity — values rooted in our Christian faith. This Privacy Policy explains clearly what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We will never sell your personal data.

1. Who We Are

Good Steward Network ("Good Steward", "we", "us") operates the website at goodstewardtv-media.github.io/GoodStewardWebsite. We are a Christian marketplace platform connecting businesses, talents and communities across Asia and the world. Contact us at: hello@goodsteward.net

2. What Data We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Company name, registration number, industry
  • Country, city, website URL
  • Profile bio, skills, social media links
  • Church affiliation (optional)
  • Account password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)

Information collected automatically:

  • Pages visited and time spent (via Google Analytics — anonymous)
  • Device type, browser, operating system
  • Approximate location (country/city level only)
  • Cookies placed by Google AdSense for relevant advertising

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your Good Steward account
  • To display your business or talent profile in our directories
  • To send you account notifications and platform updates
  • To send the weekly Faith & Business newsletter (if subscribed)
  • To improve our platform based on anonymous usage data
  • To show relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To comply with legal obligations under PDPA Malaysia and GDPR

4. Cookies & Google AdSense

We use cookies to improve your browsing experience. Google AdSense places cookies to show you relevant advertisements based on your interests. These cookies are managed by Google and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads. You can also manage cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Data Sharing

We will NEVER sell your personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Google (Analytics and AdSense — anonymised usage data)
  • Law enforcement if legally required
  • No other third parties, ever

6. Your Rights

Under PDPA Malaysia and GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time
  • Download a copy of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@goodsteward.net

7. Data Security

We take data security seriously. All passwords are encrypted. We use HTTPS for all data transmission. We limit access to your data to authorised personnel only. As a Christian platform, being a Good Steward of your data is a core part of our mission.

8. Children's Privacy

Good Steward is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered members by email of any significant changes. Continued use of the platform after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests:
